JINTORI Privacy Policy

GerryMax Studio (the “Developer”) explains how information is handled in the Android app JINTORI below.

1. Accounts and display names

The app does not provide account registration, Google account sign-in, or Google Play Games integration. A username is an optional display name stored on your device. Its default is Guest. It is not a registration of your legal name, email address, or verified identity.

2. Information stored on your device and its purposes

The app stores the following information locally to save and resume games, maintain progression, display statistics, remember settings, and manage My Stages:

  • Campaign progress, clear records, unlocks, medals, match results, and play time.
  • Boards, troop counts, inventory, turns, match settings, and undo history.
  • Language, sound, game preferences, and records of explanations already viewed.
  • Your display name, stage names, creator names, save dates, favorites, and imported stage data.
  • Identifiers used to distinguish matches and saved entries locally.

This information uses local device storage. The app does not automatically send it to the Developer’s servers or provide cloud synchronization operated by the Developer. CPU decisions and game calculations also run on the device.

3. Advertising, purchases, and automatic reporting

The app has no advertising, in-app purchasing, third-party behavioral analytics, or automatic crash-report upload features. It also has no features for obtaining advertising identifiers, location, contacts, call history, or microphone recordings.

Services credited for development assistance or music production do not indicate that the app sends gameplay information to those services.

4. Camera, images, and files

The camera is used when you choose QR scanning and grant permission. Camera frames are processed on the device to decode QR codes. The app does not send them to the Developer or automatically save them as video. QR camera scanning does not capture audio.

For image import, the app processes an image you select on your device. When you choose image saving or sharing, the app creates a QR image in device storage or temporary storage. Camera or storage permission may be requested depending on your device and OS.

You can change camera permission in OS settings. Other import methods, such as entering a password, remain available without camera permission.

5. Stage sharing and the clipboard

When you choose to share a stage, its initial board, match settings, stage name, creator name, and related stage information are included in a password or QR image. The creator name may use your display name at the time of saving. Changing your username later does not automatically change names already saved or shared.

Copying writes the selected stage password to the clipboard. Sharing passes data to the OS share feature or an app you choose. The app does not automatically upload stages to a sharing server operated by the Developer.

Stage passwords and QR codes are not encrypted secrets. Recipients can import and re-share the data. Do not include personal information you wish to keep private in display names or stage names. The handling of information by a receiving app or service is governed by that provider’s policies.

6. External services and connections

The Android app uses bundled game content and audio. It does not make external connections to collect gameplay records, usernames, or camera images for the Developer. External services you use, such as OS sharing, Google Play, and email, perform their own processing under their providers’ policies.

When you view this policy on GitHub Pages, GitHub records visitor IP addresses for security purposes. This policy website has no custom analytics, advertising, or input forms. See GitHub’s privacy statement.

For access logs specific to a web trial’s hosting provider, consult that hosting service’s policies. This policy does not guarantee a web host’s retention periods or the OS’s handling of diagnostic information.

7. Retention and deletion

Progress, statistics, preferences, and saved stages are kept on the device for the app’s save features. You can delete individual My Stages entries in the app. You can remove local app storage using an option such as “Clear storage” in Android app settings; names vary by device and OS.

Temporary sharing images are created in a cache directory and are subject to app cleanup and OS management. Images you saved, data already sent to recipients, and copies in device backups may remain after a stage is deleted in the app. Manage them through the relevant storage location, app, or OS.

The Developer does not provide cloud synchronization or Google Play Games cloud saves. OS backup and restoration behavior depends on device and OS settings. The app does not guarantee backup coverage or restoration.

The Developer cannot remotely view, restore, or delete data that exists only on your device. There is no app account and therefore no account cancellation process.

8. Information protection

Imported passwords and QR data are checked for length, integrity, board structure, and settings. Entered names are treated as text, not executable code. These checks do not guarantee encryption or a creator’s verified identity.

Please also protect your device, use appropriate screen-lock settings, and choose sharing destinations carefully. The app has no mechanism for selling personal information it handles.

9. Use by children

The app does not request a date of birth or a legal name. Parents and guardians should ensure that children do not share personal information through display names or stage names.

10. Contact and changes

Use the contact below for questions about this policy. Contact details and information you voluntarily provide in an inquiry are used to respond to it. You do not need to send all local game data or unrelated personal information.
